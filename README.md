# Near Earth Explorer

An interactive map of what's near Earth right now, covering satellites and asteroids. Click any object to see its facts, a NASA photo, and a voice-narrated story.

Built for the hackathon on Nov 7-8, 2026, by a team of four.

## What the user sees

- A half-Earth graphic with satellites close to the surface and asteroids drawn as trajectory lines.
- Two highlighted "hero" objects: the satellite closest to your location and the asteroid with the closest approach in the next 7 days.
- A search bar that also accepts plain-language questions like "What's close to Earth this week?", which Gemini interprets.
- An observer feature that says where and when to look, e.g. "southwest, about 40 degrees up, at 19:24".
